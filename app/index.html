    Platinum Roadmap

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Work the stages in order; almost everything can be done after the credits, except the one missable in Stage 1.

      Roadmap built from the official trophy descriptions. Time estimate and difficulty: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

      Missable Trophies — Watch List

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam. Because the game has no mission replay, keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

        Why the rest are safe

        PowerPyx and GTABase both flag a single trophy — Positive Mental Attitude — as potentially missable, and only because of one meditation at Jason's house in Chapter 1. The story trophies unlock from mandatory missions, the activities and collectibles stay open after the credits, and the 100% checklist has nothing missable on it. Do the Chapter 1 meditation, keep chapter-start saves, and the platinum cannot be locked out.

        Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; GTABase — GTA 6 trophy guide; Beebom — GTA 6 Meditation Spots Locations.
